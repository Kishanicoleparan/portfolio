# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

Primary users are potential employers, collaborators, and friends or clients evaluating the creator's work, skills, and personality. The visitor is likely browsing from a desktop or mobile device to learn about the portfolio owner and see their project examples.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents the creator's personal brand, projects, technical skills, hobbies, and contact details. Success means a visitor can quickly understand who the person is, what they are capable of, and how to reach them or view their work.

## Positioning

This is a personal portfolio site that showcases a student/designer/developer identity through curated content, creative presentation, and accessible contact information. Its differentiator is the combination of personal storytelling, work samples, and skill visibility in a polished and modern single-site experience.

## Operating Context

The site is a static portfolio used for personal branding and networking. Visitors may browse the home, projects, and contact pages, view skills and examples, and contact the owner through a form or external links. The experience is expected to work smoothly across common desktop and mobile screen sizes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built as a static HTML/CSS website.
- Includes a home page, portfolio/projects page, and contact page.
- Shows profile summary, work examples, skills, hobbies, and social/contact information.
- The contact form is presentational and does not connect to a backend service.
- The portfolio is personal and brand-oriented rather than a business/product SaaS interface.
- No confirmed product-specific accessibility requirements were supplied beyond standard responsive, readable web behavior.

## Brand Commitments

The portfolio identity is modern, creative, and tech-forward, with a dark, neon-accented aesthetic and clearly defined portfolio branding. The site names itself as "MyPortfolio" and uses a bold, design-oriented visual tone consistent with a student designer/developer profile.

## Evidence on Hand

- Home, project, and contact pages exist in the portfolio folder.
- Styling is defined in css/style.css and includes a dark gradient theme, purple/cyan accent palette, and responsive layout rules.
- Personal content includes name, project categories, hobbies, contact details, and social links.
- No formal brand guidelines, legal assets, or external proof materials were provided beyond the current content and styling.

## Product Principles

1. Showcase identity clearly and immediately.
2. Make work and skills easy to scan and understand.
3. Keep the experience polished, responsive, and approachable.
4. Prioritize personal credibility, clarity, and contactability.
5. Balance creative visual confidence with readable usability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site is expected to be readable and usable across device sizes, with semantic HTML structure and visible labels for contact fields. No additional accessibility constraints beyond standard inclusive web practices were confirmed.
